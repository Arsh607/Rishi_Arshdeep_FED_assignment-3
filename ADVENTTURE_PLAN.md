# Adventure Game Plan

## Storyline Overview:
This game will be set in a medieval fantasy world where the player is a knight embarking on a quest to save the kingdom. Along the way, they will face moral dilemmas, dangerous creatures, and critical decisions.

---

## Path 1: The Forest of Shadows
1. **The Starting Point**:
   - The player begins at the edge of a dark forest.
   - *Decision Point 1*:
     - **Option 1**: Enter the forest cautiously.
     - **Option 2**: Charge in boldly without hesitation.
   
   **Results of Decision**:
     - If Option 1 is chosen: The player encounters a peaceful creature who offers helpful advice.
     - If Option 2 is chosen: The player is attacked by a wild beast and loses health.

2. **The Lost Path**:
   - The player finds themselves lost in a maze of trees.
   - *Decision Point 2*:
     - **Option 1**: Follow a distant sound of water (possible help from a river spirit).
     - **Option 2**: Climb a tree to get a better view (reveals an enemy camp).

   **Results of Decision**:
     - If Option 1 is chosen: The player finds a hidden magical artifact.
     - If Option 2 is chosen: The player is caught by the enemies and must fight to escape.

---

## Path 2: The Mountain Pass
1. **The Approach**:
   - The player must scale a steep mountain to reach the castle.
   - *Decision Point 1*:
     - **Option 1**: Hire a guide to lead the way.
     - **Option 2**: Attempt to climb without help.

   **Results of Decision**:
     - If Option 1 is chosen: The player encounters a trap set by bandits.
     - If Option 2 is chosen: The player successfully climbs but loses time.

2. **The Summit**:
   - The player reaches the top and faces a dangerous creature guarding the entrance to the castle.
   - *Decision Point 2*:
     - **Option 1**: Fight the creature directly.
     - **Option 2**: Attempt to negotiate with the creature.

   **Results of Decision**:
     - If Option 1 is chosen: The player defeats the creature in battle.
     - If Option 2 is chosen: The creature helps the player, providing a shortcut.

---

## Path 3: The Underground Caverns
1. **The Descent**:
   - The player must venture into the dark caverns beneath the kingdom.
   - *Decision Point 1*:
     - **Option 1**: Use a torch to light the path.
     - **Option 2**: Rely on the sound of the footsteps echoing in the cave.

   **Results of Decision**:
     - If Option 1 is chosen: The player can see hidden traps and avoid danger.
     - If Option 2 is chosen: The player falls into a pit but finds hidden treasure.

2. **The End of the Cavern**:
   - The player reaches the final chamber of the cavern.
   - *Decision Point 2*:
     - **Option 1**: Take the ancient sword.
     - **Option 2**: Leave the sword and exit the cavern.

   **Results of Decision**:
     - If Option 1 is chosen: The player gains a powerful weapon for the final battle.
     - If Option 2 is chosen: The player leaves empty-handed but faces less danger.
